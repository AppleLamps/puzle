## What survived re-derivation

Everything load-bearing. The 484/479 prime balance, the `PRIVATEKEY…` landing at
zero-based Architect offset 479, the `YOUWON` alignment at index 21 with its
21/49/21 split, the `fae` + 9×63 structure of S570, and both prize gates with

What did not hold was the *recording* of four results, in ways that made

two silently corrupt cache bodies that the cache writer could never replace.

`solver/preregistration_integrity_audit.py` now fails a test if the class
recurs. The suite went from 44 passed / 3 failed to 52 passed.

## The single most important framing correction

**The creator says yin-yang is unreached, and this repository's narrative
sometimes reads as though it has been reached.** He has stated three times
(2023-08-06, 2025-04-28, 2026-03-03) that reaching yin-yang means the prize is
claimed the same day, in two hours, in no-time — and as of 2026-03-03 the
funded address has not moved, which is the only oracle he consults.

The 479 balance is the strongest structural result here, but it is **fitted**:
consecutive-prime assignment, zeroing semantics, and zero-based indexing are all
solver-selected. `SOLUTION.md` reads more confidently than that. When the
creator's words and this repository's conclusions conflict, the creator wins.
Practically: do not plan as though step 4 of the seven-phrase pipeline is
settled.

## Where the effort has been going wrong

Three patterns account for most of the wasted search, and all three are
recorded in the log already:

1. **Widening a family instead of finding the operation.** The corpus holds
   millions of AES trials and roughly 1.59 million distinct gated scalars. The
   bottleneck was never candidate volume; it is that no derivation produces a
   scalar matching Half's exact public key. Another sweep of the same shape has
   very low expected value.
2. **Building on fitted results as if authenticated.** Cosmic → Chain 4 is the
   worst case: unauthenticated, never named by the creator, and explicitly
   under a hard rule not to attack as the next lock — yet a large share of the
   audit corpus lives downstream of it.
3. **Acting on stale open-item wording.** `faed[94:201]` is described as
   untested in three places and has in fact been gated since v40.

## Priorities

Ordered by expected value, with the reasoning attached.

1. **Treat yin-yang as unreached and design for it directly.** Look for a
   construction on the poster and the seven-phrase operands that produces an
   obvious dual, half, or opposite object *and* continues quickly to a scalar.
   The creator's own timing claims are the acceptance criterion: if a candidate
   step needs another multi-stage cipher chain after it, it is probably not
   step 4.
2. **Re-audit 479 as possibly incomplete rather than as a milestone.** Test the
   literal readings first — windows between named anchors, transforms forced by
   the authenticated text — and abandon any variant that needs a new free
   convention. This is open-frontier item 1 and it remains the best-motivated
   pointer in the puzzle.
3. **One small sealed pass on the "close friends" hint.** 2026-07-12 is the only
   self-declared hint in the whole transcript, and it points at personal
   knowledge rather than cryptanalysis — structurally the same shape as "Half
   and Better Half" turning out to be a person. Build a *small* preregistered
   set from publicly creator-associated facts, gate it, and stop. Not open-ended
   identity research.
4. **The F-A-E sonata comparison, if a machine-readable score can be sourced.**
   The paired-list half of that lead is now closed (v45). What is left needs an
   external transcription and a preregistered mapping that fixes `h` and `i`
   before any search.

## What the yin-yang pass added

A focused pass on priority 1 is recorded in [`ATTEMPT_LOG.md`](ATTEMPT_LOG.md)
§14b. The poster's duality is now fully inventoried rather than repeatedly
re-measured: 86 = 86, the mirrored left/right counts 44/42 against 42/44, and a
newly measured rotational split in which exactly 49 of the 98 rot180 pairs
colour-invert. Only the rotations balance; the four reflections do not.

Two readings are now closed rather than open. The poster contains exactly five
distinct colours and exactly one anomalous cell, so any "two eyes of the
yin-yang" construction is dead on this artifact. And the tempting `EYE…` string
produced by offsetting the poster's row sums from 479 is an artifact of indexing
inside `PRIVATEKEY`, not a signal.

The conclusion that matters for planning: every yin-yang balance found so far is
a **property** of the artifact, not an **operation** on it. Reaching the
milestone needs a composition rule — plausibly how `yellowblueprimes` and
`matrixsumlist` feed the dual — and that rule is still missing. More hash
families over the same balances have low expected value.

## Standing acceptance rules, unchanged

Clean PKCS#7 padding, a readable English fragment, a vanity prefix, a community
label, and on-chain activity at a solver-published address are all **not**
evidence. The only gate is `solver/targets.py`, and every new family should be
sealed before it is searched.
